# Payments Policy

## Accepted payment methods
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- PayPal
- NovaMart gift cards

We do not accept cash, checks or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged
Your card is charged when your order ships, not when you place it.

## Currency and tax
All prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is added at checkout based on your shipping address.

## Payment security
NovaMart never stores your full card number. Our support team will
**never** ask for your card number, PIN or password.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, your order is held for 48 hours. Update your
payment method in your account to keep the order; after that it is cancelled.
