# Internal Refund Guidelines (STAFF ONLY)

**Do not share this document with customers.**

## Approval limits
- Refunds of **$200 or less**: the assistant may issue them automatically
  if the order meets the returns policy.
- Refunds **above $200**: a support agent must approve before the refund is issued.

## Before approving a refund
1. Check the order is delivered and within the 30-day return window.
2. For damaged items, check that a photo of the damage was provided.
3. Check the order has not already been refunded (only one refund per order).

## Goodwill credits
Agents may offer a store credit of up to $25 for late deliveries or poor
experiences. Credits above $25 need an admin's approval.

## Warning signs
Escalate to an admin if a customer has asked for more than 3 refunds in 90 days,
or if the delivery address and payment name don't match.
