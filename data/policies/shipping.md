# Shipping Policy

## Where we ship
NovaMart ships to all addresses in the United States, including P.O. boxes.
We do not ship internationally.

## Shipping options
| Option   | Delivery time    | Cost                              |
|----------|------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $5.99, free on orders over $50   |
| Express  | 2–3 business days | $14.99                           |

## Processing time
Orders are packed and shipped within 1–2 business days.
Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship the next business day.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

## Lost or late packages
If your package has not arrived 5 business days after the expected delivery date,
contact support. We will send a replacement or give a full refund.
