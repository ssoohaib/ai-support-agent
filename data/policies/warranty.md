# Warranty Policy

## Coverage
All electronics sold by NovaMart come with a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date.
Accessories (cables, cases, chargers) have a **90-day** warranty.

## What is covered
- Defects in materials or manufacturing.
- Parts that stop working under normal use.

## What is not covered
- Accidental damage, such as drops, cracks or water damage.
- Normal wear and tear, such as scratches.
- Items that were repaired or modified by someone other than NovaMart.

## How to make a claim
Contact support with your order number, a short description of the problem,
and a photo or video if possible. If the claim is approved, we repair or
replace the item for free. If we can't do either, we give a full refund.
