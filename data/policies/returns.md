# Returns Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within **30 days** of delivery.

## What can be returned
- Items must be **unused**, in their original packaging, with all parts included.
- **Damaged or defective items are always accepted**, even if they were used
  or the packaging is open. Please send a photo of the damage.
- Gift cards and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

## How to return
1. Contact support with your order number and the reason for the return.
2. We email you a prepaid return label.
3. Drop the package at any carrier location.

## Return shipping cost
- Damaged, defective or wrong item: return shipping is free.
- Changed your mind: we take $5.99 off your refund for the return label.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to your original payment method within **5–7 business days**
after we receive and check the returned item.
